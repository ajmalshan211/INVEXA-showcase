01 / Overview

One clear workspace for every invoice.

INVEXA brings business details, clients, invoices, tax logic, downloadable PDF output, and payment status into a single focused workflow. Its goal is to make routine invoicing feel simple without hiding the information a GST invoice requires.

03 / GST Logic

The right tax structure, explained visibly.

INVEXA compares the business state with the client state and applies the appropriate GST structure. The calculation is presented clearly in the app and in the generated PDF so the user can understand how the total was formed.

i

INVEXA is an independent software project. Production use will require final compliance review by a qualified GST professional.

⌖ Same-state supply

CGST + SGST

When the supplier and client are registered in the same state, the tax is divided between Central GST and State GST.

Example on ₹10,000 at 18%
CGST ₹900
SGST ₹900
Total ₹11,800
↗ Interstate supply

IGST

When the supplier and client are registered in different states, Integrated GST is applied as a single tax amount.

Example on ₹10,000 at 18%
IGST ₹1,800
Total ₹11,800
